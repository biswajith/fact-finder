[p4.h1] REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
[p4.h2] 1.0 PURPOSE
[p4.1] The County of Ulster is currently seeking proposals for actuarial services for the Ulster County Self-Insurance Plan which includes County Government, local municipalities, school districts, and volunteer fire fighters.
[p4.2] The County is seeking a qualified Actuary Firm to provide an annual actuarial valuation of the County’s self-insured workers compensation program.
[p4.3] The County is asking for an estimate of the liability annually for a three-year period ending December 31, 2026, 2027, and 2028 with two optional one-year renewals for years ending December 31, 2029 and 2030.
[p4.4] Each year, the awarded vendor will produce a report containing a three-year forecast.
[p4.5] The County currently has 1,609 employees (1,426 full time and 183 part time), Ulster County Community College has 342 employees (181 full time and 161 part time), and there are approximately 941 active volunteer firepersons with approximately 5,745 government/local education employees.
[p4.6] There were 365 open workers’ compensation claims as of May 31, 2026.
[p4.7] The Actuarial Services for the Ulster County Self-Insurance Plan includes Plan Participants listed in Attachment A. The Employers’ Liability Report 2026 for the year ending 2025 is shown in Attachment B. Open and closed claims reports for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (through June 30, 2026) are included as Attachments C, D, E, and F, respectively.
[p4.h3] 2.0 SCOPE OF WORK
[p4.8] In accordance with the provisions of the New York State Workers’ Compensation Law, Ulster County established a pooled Self-Insurance Plan by Local Law #1 of 1979.
[p4.9] The law was amended under Local Law #10 of 2008.
[p4.10] The plan provides coverage to employees of the County, 20 towns, 3 villages, 7 school districts, 26 volunteer fire districts within the County, 1 city, and Ulster County Community College.
[p4.11] The administration of this plan is accomplished with County employees in conjunction with a Third-party administrator and is overseen by the County Executive.
[p4.12] Workers’ Compensation Actuarial Services will consist of an independent actuarial analysis of the loss reserve (incurred but not reported) related to the Workers’ Compensation Coverage.
[p4.13] The study must provide an estimate of the total reserves as of the end of the year on both a discounted and undiscounted basis.
[p4.14] The Actuary must be able to provide the following additional services to the fund:
[p4.15] •Estimate of the expected losses for the upcoming calendar year by agency.
[p4.16] •Estimate of the future loss payments by calendar year for the next two (2) years.
[p4.17] •Calculation of discounted and non-discounted unpaid liabilities at year’s end.
[p4.18] •Calculation for each individual participant to cover open liabilities if they withdraw from the plan.
[p4.19] •Anticipated and unanticipated contingencies.
[p5.1] •Allocation of reserves and/or funding to individual plan participants.
[p5.2] •Estimate of necessary reserves to include a provision for both known claims and claims that have been incurred but not reported (IBNR).
[p5.3] •Optional Services: To be performed at the request of the County and/or Plan Administrator.
[p5.4] The selected Respondent may be asked to provide additional actuarial services from time to time during the contract period.
[p5.5] This work would be performed on an hourly fee basis.
[p5.6] Please provide your hourly fee rates.
[p5.7] The preparation of the report shall include a presentation of preliminary findings in draft form, discussion of the draft report with the county, and the preparation of the final report.
[p5.8] The County may request a conference call or meeting to present and discuss results.

[p42.1] Pursuant to State Finance Law §165-a, on August 10, 2012 the Commissioner of the Office of General Services (OGS) posted a prohibited entities list of "persons" who are engaged in "investment activities in Iran" (both are defined terms in the law) on the OGS website at: http://www.ogs.ny.gov/about/regs/docs/ListofEntities.pdf By submitting a bid in response to this solicitation or by assuming the responsibility of a Contract awarded hereunder, each Bidder/Contractor, any person signing on behalf of any Bidder/Contractor and any assignee or subcontractor and, in the case of a joint bid, each party thereto, certifies, under penalty of perjury, that once the Prohibited Entities List is posted on the OGS website, that to the best of its knowledge and belief, that each Bidder/Contractor and any subcontractor or assignee is not identified on the Prohibited Entities List created pursuant to SFL § 165-a(3)(b).
[p42.2] Additionally, Bidder/Contractor is advised that once the Prohibited Entities List is posted on the OGS Website, any Bidder/Contractor seeking to renew or extend a Contract or assume the responsibility of a Contract awarded in response to this solicitation must certify at the time the Contract is renewed, extended or assigned that it is not included on the Prohibited Entities List.
[p42.3] During the term of the Contract, should the County receive information that a Bidder/Contractor is in violation of the above-referenced certification, the County will offer the person or entity an opportunity to respond.

[p42.5] The County reserves the right to reject any bid or request for assignment for a Bidder/Contractor that appears on the Prohibited Entities List prior to the award of a contract and to pursue a responsibility review with respect to any Bidder/Contractor that is awarded a contract and subsequently appears on the Prohibited Entities List.
