# Travel and expenses policy

## Booking

All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal.
Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy class.
Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy.
Business class needs written approval from a vice president before booking.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 EUR in most cities.
In London, Paris, New York and Tokyo the limit is 260 EUR per night.
Breakfast may be included in the room rate; other hotel meals are claimed as meals.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance is 60 EUR when travelling.
Alcohol is not reimbursed.
Client dinners are exempt from the daily allowance but need the client's name on the claim.

## Claims

Submit expense claims within 30 days of returning from the trip.
Every item above 25 EUR needs a receipt.
Claims submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed.
