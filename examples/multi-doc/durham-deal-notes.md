## Account background

- Oracle was picked in phase one; this RFP buys only the implementation partner. Info-Tech Research Group runs the selection for the City.
- Scope is wide: ERP, HCM with payroll, EPM, and Oracle Utilities Customer Cloud Service for water billing. Today almost everything runs on Tyler Munis, plus Kronos, NeoGov and a long tail.
- About 2,900 employees, so HCM and payroll are the biggest piece of work. Utility billing is the riskiest.
- Award goes to the City Manager or Council after negotiation, so the start date depends on their calendar.

## Hot buttons

1. Utility billing done right. Kevin will decide whether he trusts us.
2. Payroll live on a January 1, for a clean tax year.
3. A phased plan with City staff time spelled out; they are thin.

## Win themes

1. Utility billing with Lakemont: two Oracle utilities go-lives between us.
2. North Carolina public-sector team with payroll and tax experience.
3. A realistic phasing: finance and procurement, then HCM and payroll, then utility billing.

## Competition

- The two national integrators have bigger municipal lists but weak utility billing references.
- Brightline is regional and cheap. Our Oracle rep says they are pricing around $9M. Not confirmed. Do not use.

## Pricing

- Target $7.5-8.2M, fixed price by phase. Utility billing is where we carry contingency.
- Our standard price validity is 90 days; Legal to check against the RFP.
- Termination for convenience on 30 days' notice: Legal wants it as an exception.

## Risks

- **Mandatory references.** The RFP needs Oracle work for at least two municipalities of 100k-500k in the last five years. Riverbend (210k) counts. Pine Hollow is 82k, so it doesn't. Options: Lakemont's municipal client (165k) through teaming, or our county project, which is not a municipality. Decide by 8/9.
- Utility billing scope and data quality.
- Council timing could push the start into 2025 and squeeze the January 2026 payroll date.
