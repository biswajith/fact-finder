A POINT IN NOTES
Notes are our own view of the deal, written fast and in shorthand.
- A CRM field row with a value is a point; the field name says what the value means. A field name with no value states no point.
- Our own guesses, estimates and hopes are points. Flag them tentative.
- A to-do, a follow-up, or a promise to someone is ACTION.
- Shorthand, initials, and abbreviations are read for what they stand for; never correct or complete a number or a date.
- A heading alone, a date stamp alone, and a divider state no point.
- A line that only continues or qualifies the line before it belongs with that line.
